SUNFLOWER

Sentuh sticky notes di buketnya ✨

Your eyes are morning dew,

your smile is sunlight,

and your presence

feels like spring.

If my heart were a garden,

I'd let every flower

grow around you

You are my sunflower,

turning every quiet morning

toward the light.

And somehow,

whenever I see you,

my heart does the same.
